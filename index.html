Welcome to Kondo's Bakery

Things have gotten a bit out of hand and now pastries (and who knows what else) are falling from the ceiling. It is up to you to clean up the mess!

How to Play

Catch

Use the left and right arrow keys to move and collect as many baked goods as you can!

Keep an eye on that special pastel de nata. It's worth extra points, so don't let it slip away!

Avoid

Kitchen Knife: One touch and it's instant game over!

Burnt Pastries: Catch 5 and you're done!

Moldy Items: They are a serious health hazard...Catch 3 and you're out.
