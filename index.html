Kondor's Bakery

Welcome to Kondor's Bakery! Things have gotten a bit out of hand and now pastries (and who knows what else) are falling from the ceiling. It is up to you to clean up the mess! Yes, you!

How to Play

Catch

Your mission is to collect as many baked goods as you can, use your left and right arrow keys to move.

Keep an eye on that special cupcake. It's worth extra points, so don't let it slip away!

Avoid

Whatever you do, don't catch the kitchen knife! Touch it and it's an instant game over!

Not all baked goods are created equal. Each burnt item costs you 1 point. Catch 5 and it's game over!

Not everything that falls is worth catching! Stay away from the moldy items, they are a serious health hazard...Catch 3 and you're out.

SWEET VICTORY

Every pastry is in its place.

Kondor's Bakery is ready for the morning rush!
