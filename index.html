Kondo's Bakery

Welcome to Kondo's Bakery! Things have gotten a bit out of hand and now pastries (and who knows what else) are falling from the ceiling. It is up to you to clean up the mess! Yes, you!

How to Play

Catch

Your mission is to collect as many baked goods as you can, use your left and right arrow keys to move.

Keep an eye on that special pastel de nata. It's worth extra points, so don't let it slip away!

Avoid

Not everything that falls is worth catching!

Kitchen Knife: One touch and it's instant game over!

Burnt Pastries: Catch 5 and you're done!

Moldy Items: They are a serious health hazard...Catch 3 and you're out.
